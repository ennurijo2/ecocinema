# About Ecocinema Map

## Summary

The Ecocinema Map shows movies and other moving image media texts that invite viewers to engage with the natural environment on screen. The points on the map show either the shooting location where the film was made or the diegetic location where the narrative of the film takes place. 

## Who made it?

The map is a collaborative creation of Loyola Marymount University's Film/Media and Social Justice course on Ecocinema (Fall 2026). Each entry was written by a student. 

The framework for the map is a creation of <a href="https://ennurijo.com">Ennuri Jo</a> (she/her), a film and media studies scholar and professor based in Los Angeles. For more information, check out its parent project Media Mapper (funded by the <a href="https://www.asc.upenn.edu/research/centers/center-for-advanced-research-in-global-communication">Center for Advanced Research in Global Communication</a> at the University of Pennsylvania).
